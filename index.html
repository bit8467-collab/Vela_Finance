01 THE PROJECT

You Invest.

A database-backed account platform with public market reference data.

Registered accounts, balance records, requests, and tracked portfolios are stored by the platform backend. Asset prices and charts are sourced from public market-data providers and may be delayed or unavailable.

Market tracking positions do not mean that the platform has bought or holds Bitcoin, gold, shares, or other assets.

02 LIMITATIONS

Services and permissions.

No broker, exchange, bank, payment processor, or blockchain settlement integration is configured.

Deposits and withdrawals are requests recorded for manual review; submitting a request does not move cryptocurrency or issue a payout. A stop threshold is evaluated against public reference quotes when the account refreshes, not continuously and not as a market order. No regulatory approval, legal entity, company office, certificate, or support contact is asserted here. This is not financial advice.

iPLATFORM STATUSMarket data and limitationsWhat this account platform does and does not do

Accounts, requests and tracking positions are stored in the platform database. Market quotes are public reference data. The platform is not connected to a broker, exchange, payment processor or blockchain settlement service, and it does not buy or custody the listed assets.

Stop threshold

A threshold is checked when the signed-in account refreshes and market data is available. It updates the internal tracking ledger; it is not a continuous exchange order.

Deposits and withdrawals
